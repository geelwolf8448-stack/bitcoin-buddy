Bitcoin basics

A quick, beginner-friendly starting point.

  1. Bitcoin (BTC) is a digital asset that operates on a public network.
  2. A wallet helps you manage the keys used to access Bitcoin. Keep recovery phrases private.
  3. Volatility means the price can rise or fall quickly, including losing substantial value.
  4. Scams often promise guaranteed returns. No legitimate investment can guarantee Bitcoin profits.
