Bitcoin Buddy is an independent educational project for people who are new to Bitcoin. We are looking for an industry partner to support a transparent, real-world mining demonstration.

The partnership we're seeking

We are requesting a fully funded 12-month pilot. The sponsor would provide or lend suitable ASIC mining equipment and cover every operating cost for the agreed period.

Hardware

One suitable ASIC miner, supplied or loaned for the pilot.

Hosting & power

Secure hosting and all electricity costs paid directly by the sponsor.

Operations

Maintenance, repairs, insurance and any required operating costs.

What the sponsor receives

  • Clearly disclosed sponsor recognition on Bitcoin Buddy.
  • Educational updates explaining the equipment, mining process and measured results.
  • Agreed promotional exposure and a final pilot summary.
  • Honest reporting, including downtime, costs and results; no guaranteed-return claims.

Our non-negotiable condition

No payment from the project founder. We cannot contribute deposits, purchase costs, loan instalments, electricity bills, hosting charges, activation fees or withdrawal fees. Any proposal must cover all costs in full and be documented in a written agreement before equipment is accepted.

About the project

Bitcoin Buddy is an educational website, not a mining pool, investment product, exchange or wallet. The proposed demonstration is intended to teach visitors how Bitcoin mining works in practice. Mining rewards and profitability are not guaranteed.
