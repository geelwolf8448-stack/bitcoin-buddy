Bitcoin basics

Three things to understand before you go further.

🌐

What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a digital asset that operates on a public network. Transactions are recorded on a shared ledger called a blockchain.

📉

Understand the risk

Its price can rise or fall dramatically. You could lose some or all of the money you invest. Never borrow money to buy crypto.

🔐

Protect yourself

Never share your password or recovery phrase. Be suspicious of guaranteed returns, urgent messages, and requests to send crypto.
