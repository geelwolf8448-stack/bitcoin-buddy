Lesson 1 — What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a digital asset that can be transferred over the internet on a network maintained by computers around the world. Its transaction history is recorded on a public ledger called the blockchain.

Bitcoin is not a bank account, a company share or a guaranteed way to make money. Its price can change sharply.

Lesson 2 — Wallets and safety

A wallet helps you manage the cryptographic keys used to authorise Bitcoin transactions. A recovery phrase can restore access to a wallet, so anyone who obtains it may be able to take the funds.

Never share your recovery phrase. Be careful with links, fake support accounts and lookalike websites. Check addresses carefully before sending.

Lesson 3 — Transactions and fees

Bitcoin transactions are broadcast to the network and confirmed through its consensus process. Confirmation can take time, and network fees may vary.

Before using any service, read its fees, withdrawal rules and terms. Bitcoin Buddy does not connect to an exchange or process transactions.

Lesson 4 — Scams and risk

Be wary of guaranteed returns, pressure to act quickly, unexpected giveaway links, upfront withdrawal fees and requests for your recovery phrase.

Pause and verify claims independently. Never borrow money or risk money needed for essentials. If something sounds too good to be true, walk away.

This is general education, not financial advice or a qualification.
